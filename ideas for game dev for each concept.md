## 2\. Chapter 4 — Timeline and Sources of History

**NCERT's own Big Questions:** How do we measure historical time? How can various sources help us understand history? How did early humans live?

> **Design note:** 4.27 is the intellectual heart of the whole chapter and the most game-shaped idea in it — two sources disagree, you decide who to believe. It is also the one thing a quiz app cannot do and a game can. If the dig turns up a text saying one thing and a pot saying another, and the player must rule, you have taught the actual discipline of history. NCERT's own end-of-chapter question 2 is "Can we compare historians to detectives?" They are asking for this game.
